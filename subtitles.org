* subtitles - version 1.14.1 - description

subtitles --- a GNU shell script that turns a film's dubbed audio track
              (Japanese by default) into proper subtitles of what is
              actually said, using a local Whisper model on the GPU,
              guided by an existing but textually wrong subtitle file
              whose timings are good (a translation track, an OCR of a
              bitmap track, an auto-generated track, …).

Copyright (C) 2026 白い熊

Author: 白い熊

** Why
A disc's Japanese subtitle track is a translation of the *original*
dialogue — it is not what the Japanese dub says.  OCR of such a track
gives good cue timings and useless text.  This tool keeps the timings
as a map of where speech is, transcribes the dub with Whisper, and
builds subtitles from the dub's own word timestamps.

** Pipeline
1. Decode the audio to 16 kHz mono WAV (=ffmpeg=, any container), from
   the container's time 0 — a track that starts late is padded.  Of a
   surround track only the centre channel, where the dialogue is.  The
   check track is chosen too: the original's subtitles, by default
   the fullest text track in the language of another audio stream.
2. Speech regions: the reference SRT's cues, padded 0.4 s before and
   0.6 s after and merged across gaps of ≤ 1 s, united with anything
   a VAD (Silero) hears outside them.  Without a reference SRT the VAD
   alone defines the regions.  Regions longer than 60 s are split at
   their largest internal gap.
3. Whisper large-v3, per region, with word timestamps — *twice*: once
   with a prompt of character names (better spellings), once without.
   The prompt makes Whisper occasionally emit the prompt text instead
   of the audio, so the two passes are merged region by region: the
   prompted result only where it is hallucination-free and covers the
   VAD speech at least as well as the unprompted one.
4. Filters: prompt echoes, the classic YouTube-outro hallucinations
   (「ご視聴ありがとうございました」, "Thanks for watching"), repetition
   loops, no-speech segments.  Name variants are normalised.
5. Speakers (on by default, =-d off= to skip): VAD phrases get a voice
   embedding (ECAPA-TDNN, SpeechBrain), long phrases are clustered over
   the film, short ones join the nearest voice heard nearby; a change
   of voice inside a cue is placed at a legal token boundary near the
   silence, and is where the cue's two lines part.  Reliable on a
   scene, less so over a whole film — see the changelog.
6. Assembly, driven by the reference.  The words are apportioned to
   the reference cues at the best legal token boundary within ±1 s of
   each reference edge — sentence punctuation or a pause preferred,
   never inside a word.  Then whole sentences: neighbouring cues whose
   shared edge falls inside one sentence of the dub are joined, and a
   chain too long for one cue is cut at a sentence end, else a clause
   end — never merely where the reference's cue ended.  A cue's timing follows its speech:
   it starts at the VAD phrase holding its first word (the reference
   start when that is earlier) and ends 0.4 s after the phrase holding
   its last word ends — the reference end is only a floor, since the
   disc's cues are authored to the original language's lines.  Two
   sentences, or a turn, inside one reference cue go on two lines, as
   on the disc; longer text breaks at punctuation or a particle, never
   inside a katakana or kanji run; ≤ 2 lines (16 characters for
   Japanese, 42 for Latin scripts), else the cue is split in two.
   Speech the reference has no cue for is cut at pauses ≥ 0.7 s and
   sentence ends into cues of its own.  Without a reference everything
   goes that way.
   Then, by default, the cues are regrouped into one whole sentence
   each (=-g sentence=), wrapped into as many lines as it needs.
7. Cross-check (=-k=, Japanese): every line is read against the
   original's subtitles — a name the original says that the line lacks
   but sounds like (Kyle heard as 帰る), a meaning (LaBSE) or a number
   that does not match.  Candidates are decoded again and scored by
   Whisper against the audio; only what the audio supports replaces the
   line.  Confirmed corrections of a person's go in a corrections file
   (=-x=), applied before the assembly.
8. Checks: every speech cue of the check track that no Whisper
   segment covers is transcribed again on its own window (before the
   assembly), and the check is repeated on the written cues; cues still
   without a line, VAD speech left without a line and the
   lowest-confidence lines go into a review report beside the SRT.

** Lessons baked in (from the first use, The Thin Red Line, 2026-09-16)
- The reference SRT and the audio *must be on the same timeline*; a
  Blu-ray with a longer head shifts everything (there it was 10.823 s).
  Check the first cue against the first line of dialogue before running.
- A raw =.aac= extracted from an MKV decodes with its 1024-sample
  priming in place, so it is timeline-correct as a standalone file.
- A bitmap (PGS) subtitle track needs no OCR to serve as the timing
  reference: its packets are "show" (large, with a duration) and
  "clear" (≈30 bytes) display sets, which is all a cue map needs.
- Two passes are not optional: with the prompt alone 12 of 32 minutes
  of speech came out as prompt echo; without it names drift
  (ウイット/ウィット).  Merged: 0.2 minutes uncovered.
- Whisper's Japanese has ordinary homophone slips (階級賞/階級章,
  軍装/軍曹); the result is proofreading material, not a final text.

** Usage
#+begin_example
subtitles.sh [options] INPUT            INPUT: any audio or video file ffmpeg reads
  -h            this help
  -p            PROBE: run every probe and print a verdict per option — green
                when the current value is right, yellow for a suggestion, red
                when a change is needed — plus the ready-made next command
  -P WHAT       one probe only: streams|timeline|regions|language|models|names
  -l LANG       language of the dub (default ja)
  -r REF.srt    reference subtitles with usable timings (default: the fullest
                subtitle track inside INPUT, PGS or text; "-r none" for VAD only)
  -s SECONDS    shift REF.srt by this much before use (default 0)
  -n NAMES      character names for the prompt, comma-separated (default none:
                single unprompted pass); a name may carry variants: 正=誤1|誤2
  -m MODEL      Whisper model (default large-v3)
  -a N          audio stream index inside a container (default: the first
                stream tagged with LANG, else 0)
  -o OUT.srt    output (default INPUT-basename.LANG.srt beside the input)
  -w DIR        work dir with the cache (default /tmp/subtitles/BASENAME)
  -f            force re-transcription, ignore cached passes
  -d on|off     speaker separation (default on): two speakers in one cue go
                on separate lines
  -v CHECK.srt  the original's subtitles: every line of it must get a line
                (default: the fullest text track inside INPUT in the language
                of another audio stream; "-v none" for no check)
  -c CHANNEL    auto|center|mix: decode only the centre channel of a surround
                dub, where the dialogue is (default auto: when it has one)
  -M            update the input MKV in place: the SRT goes in before the
                first subtitle track of LANG (text before bitmap), after all
                subtitles when there is none; verified before replacing; the
                SRT and the review then move into the work dir (nothing is
                left beside the movie)
  -S N          sample regions for the probes (default 12)
  -L MODELS     models tried by the models probe (default turbo,large-v3,medium)
#+end_example
The simple workflow: run once with =-p=, read the verdict, then run
once more without =-p= — with the options the verdict recommended, if
any.  Every pass shows "region xx/XXX" with elapsed and remaining time.
With an MKV as input nothing else is needed: the audio stream tagged
with the dub language and the fullest subtitle track (a bitmap PGS
track serves as well — only its timings are read) are picked and
listed, each marked "this one will be used".
#+begin_example
subtitles.sh -p "Dark knight, the -- [7][227] (2008) 2160p.mkv"
subtitles.sh -M -n "バットマン,ジョーカー,ゴードン" "Dark knight, the -- [7][227] (2008) 2160p.mkv"
subtitles.sh -p -r japanese-wrong.srt thin-red-line.aac
#+end_example

** Runtime dependencies
- =ffmpeg=, =mkvmerge= (for -M), =python3= with a CUDA-enabled PyTorch
  installed system-wide (torch 2.13+cu130 on kumatux).
- A venv on top of the system PyTorch, created automatically on first
  run at =$KXWHISPERVENV= (default =~/.venvs/whisper=):
  =python3 -m venv --system-site-packages ~/.venvs/whisper && ~/.venvs/whisper/bin/pip install openai-whisper silero-vad speechbrain fugashi unidic-lite scipy=
  (fugashi + unidic-lite = MeCab, for Japanese word boundaries)
- The Whisper model (large-v3, 2.9 GB) is downloaded on first use into
  =~/.cache/whisper=; the ECAPA speaker model (80 MB) into the work
  dir's parent =/tmp/subtitles/models=.

** 1.11.0
*** 2026-10-18_16-13-40 UTC (日曜日)
**** Two speakers in one cue are marked: each line begins with the language's dash (Japanese "- ", the half-width hyphen and a space, as in Netflix's Japanese style guide — a long-vowel ー at a line's start would read as part of the word). def srt_time(s):
    h, m, rest = s.strip().split(":"); sec, ms = rest.split(",")
    return int(h)*3600 + int(m)*60 + int(sec) + int(ms)/1000

def fmt_time(t):
    ms = max(0, int(round(t*1000)))
    return "%02d:%02d:%02d,%03d" % (ms//3600000, (ms//60000) % 60, (ms//1000) % 60, ms % 1000)

def hms(s): return "%d:%02d:%05.2f" % (s//3600, (s % 3600)//60, s % 60)

**** Files
Positioning tags such as ={\an8}= are dropped on reading; =shift= moves
every cue (positive = later), which is how a reference on another
timeline is brought onto the audio's.  A track for the deaf times its
sound descriptions like speech — =[CAT HISSING]= between two lines of
dialogue took the end of the first (Terminator 3, 2026-10-17) — so a
reference cue holding nothing but bracketed descriptions, song lyrics
(♪) or a speaker label is no speech cue and is dropped.
#+begin_src python
def read_srt(path, shift=0.0):
    """-> list of (start, end, text)"""
    txt = open(path, encoding="utf-8-sig", errors="replace").read()
    out = []
    for b in re.split(r"\n\s*\n", txt.strip()):
        lines = b.strip().split("\n")
        if len(lines) < 2 or "-->" not in lines[1]: continue
        a, c = lines[1].split("-->")
        text = re.sub(r"\{\\an\d\}", "", "\n".join(lines[2:])).strip()
        out.append((srt_time(a)+shift, srt_time(c)+shift, text))
    return [c for c in out if c[1] > 0]

# a cue of a subtitle track for the deaf that holds no speech: [SIREN WAILING], (laughs), ♪ lyrics ♪, a bare speaker label
def sound_only(text):
    t = re.sub(r"</?[^>]+>", "", text)
    t = re.sub(r"\[[^\]]*\]|\([^)]*\)", "", t)
    lines = [l for l in t.split("\n") if "♪" not in l and not l.strip().startswith("#")]
    t = re.sub(r"^\s*-?\s*([A-Z][A-Z0-9 .'-]*:)?", "", "\n".join(lines), flags=re.M)
    return not re.search(r"\w", t)

def speech_cues(cues): return [c for c in cues if not sound_only(c[2])]

def write_srt(path, cues):
    with open(path, "w", encoding="utf-8") as f:
        f.write("\n".join("%d\n%s --> %s\n%s\n" % (i, fmt_time(s), fmt_time(e), t)
                          for i, (s, e, t) in enumerate(cues, 1)))

**** Region assembly
The padded reference cues, plus VAD speech that no padded cue covers,

#+begin_src python
def outside_cues(cues, vad, total):
    cov = np.zeros(int(total)+2)
    for s, e, _ in cues: cov[max(0, int(s-PAD_BEFORE)):int(e+PAD_AFTER)+1] = 1
    return [(s, e) for s, e in vad if cov[int(s)] == 0 and cov[int(e)] == 0 and e-s >= VAD_MIN_OUTSIDE]

def build_regions(cues, vad, total):
    iv = [(max(0, s-PAD_BEFORE), min(total, e+PAD_AFTER)) for s, e, _ in cues]
    iv += [(max(0, s-0.3), min(total, e+0.3)) for s, e in outside_cues(cues, vad, total)]
    iv.sort(); regions = []
    for s, e in iv:
        if regions and s - regions[-1][1] <= MERGE_GAP: regions[-1][1] = max(regions[-1][1], e)
        else: regions.append([s, e])
    return [r for reg in regions for r in split_long(reg, cues, vad)]
#+end_src
**** Splitting long regions
Whisper works in 30-s windows; a region longer than =MAX_REGION= is cut
at its largest internal gap between cues (or VAD chunks).  =MAX_REGION=
stays below the window: when a region spans two windows, Whisper can
lose the thread at the seek to the second, skip whole lines and stretch
a single token over them.
#+begin_src python
def split_long(r, cues, vad):
    s, e = r
    if e-s <= MAX_REGION: return [r]
    inner = sorted(c[:2] for c in cues if s < c[0] and c[1] < e) or sorted(v for v in vad if s < v[0] and v[1] < e)
    gaps = [(inner[i+1][0]-inner[i][1], (inner[i][1]+inner[i+1][0])/2) for i in range(len(inner)-1)]
    if not gaps: return [r]
    g, m = max(gaps)
    return split_long([s, m], cues, vad) + split_long([m, e], cues, vad)

def mmss(t): return "%d:%02d" % (t//60, t % 60)

class Progress:
    def __init__(self, label, total):
        self.label, self.total, self.t0 = label, total, time.time(); self.tty = sys.stdout.isatty()
    def step(self, i, extra=""):
        el = time.time()-self.t0; rem = el/(i)*(self.total-i) if i else 0
        line = "  %s %*d/%d  elapsed %s  remaining ~%s  %s" % (self.label, len(str(self.total)), i, self.total, mmss(el), mmss(rem), extra)
        if self.tty: print("\r" + line + " "*8, end="", flush=True)
        elif i % 25 == 0 or i == self.total: print(line, flush=True)
    def done(self):
        if self.tty: print(flush=True)

**** One pass over regions
One Whisper call per region; the region's start is added back to every
segment and word time.  The result is cached per pass, keyed by each
region's bounds, so that the subtitle-building parameters can be
re-tuned without re-transcribing, and a change of regions transcribes
only the regions not seen before.  A cache of the old format (a list
indexed by region number) is converted with the previous
=regions.json=, entry by entry where its segments fit their region.
#+begin_src python
def region_key(s, e): return "%.3f-%.3f" % (s, e)

def load_pass_cache(cache, old_regions=None):
    """-> {region key: segments}"""
    if not (cache and os.path.exists(cache)): return {}
    d = json.load(open(cache))
    if isinstance(d, dict): return d.get("regions", {})
    by = {}
    if old_regions:
        for seg in d:
            by.setdefault(seg["region"], []).append(seg)
        by = {region_key(*old_regions[k]): v for k, v in by.items()
              if k < len(old_regions) and all(old_regions[k][0] - 1 <= g["start"] and g["end"] <= old_regions[k][1] + 1 for g in v)}
    return by

def save_pass_cache(cache, data):
    tmp = cache + ".tmp"; json.dump(dict(version=2, regions=data), open(tmp, "w"), ensure_ascii=False); os.replace(tmp, cache)

def transcribe(x, sr, regions, lang, model_name, prompt, cache=None, force=False, model=None, label="regions", old_regions=None):
    data = {} if force else load_pass_cache(cache, old_regions)
    missing = [k for k, (s, e) in enumerate(regions) if region_key(s, e) not in data]
    if not missing: print("  %s: cached (%s)" % (label, os.path.basename(cache) if cache else "-"))
    elif cache and len(missing) < len(regions): print("  %s: %d of %d cached, transcribing %d" % (label, len(regions) - len(missing), len(regions), len(missing)), flush=True)
    own = model is None
    if missing:
        prog = Progress(label, len(missing))
        for i, k in enumerate(missing):
            if model is None: model = load_model(model_name)
            s, e = regions[k]
            r = model.transcribe(x[int(s*sr):int(e*sr)], language=lang, task="transcribe",
                                 word_timestamps=True, initial_prompt=prompt,
                                 condition_on_previous_text=False, fp16=True, beam_size=5, best_of=5)
            data[region_key(s, e)] = [dict(region=k, start=s+seg["start"], end=s+seg["end"], text=seg["text"],
                                           avg_logprob=seg["avg_logprob"], no_speech_prob=seg["no_speech_prob"],
                                           compression_ratio=seg["compression_ratio"],
                                           words=[dict(w=w["word"], start=s+w["start"], end=s+w["end"], p=w.get("probability", 0))
                                                  for w in seg.get("words", [])]) for seg in r["segments"]]
            prog.step(i+1, "at %s, %d segments" % (hms(s), len(data[region_key(s, e)])))
            if cache and i % 10 == 0: save_pass_cache(cache, data)
        prog.done()
        if cache: save_pass_cache(cache, data)
    if own and model is not None: unload_model(model)
    out = []
    for k, (s, e) in enumerate(regions):
        for seg in data[region_key(s, e)]: out.append(dict(seg, region=k, words=[dict(w) for w in seg["words"]]))
    return out

**** Repairing skipped speech
Should Whisper still skip speech inside a region, it shows as one
token stretched over the lost lines: a token longer than =SKIP_TOKEN=
with at least =SKIP_SPEECH= of VAD speech under it.  The segment
holding it is transcribed again as a region of its own (padded, cut
below =MAX_REGION= if need be, cached like any region); the new
segments inside the old one's span replace it when they leave less
speech under stretched tokens.
#+begin_src python
SKIP_TOKEN, SKIP_SPEECH = 2.0, 1.0

def vad_under(s, e, vad): return sum(max(0.0, min(e, b) - max(s, a)) for a, b in vad)

def skipped(segs, vad):
    """seconds of VAD speech under stretched tokens"""
    return sum(vad_under(w["start"], w["end"], vad) for g in segs for w in g["words"]
               if w["end"] - w["start"] > SKIP_TOKEN and vad_under(w["start"], w["end"], vad) >= SKIP_SPEECH)

def repair_skips(x, sr, segs, cues, vad, lang, model_name, prompt, cache, label):
    """-> segments with the repaired ones replaced, and the repairs [(start, end, old text, new text, accepted)]"""
    bad = [i for i, g in enumerate(segs) if skipped([g], vad) > 0]
    if not bad: return segs, []
    wins = {i: split_long([max(0.0, segs[i]["start"] - PAD_BEFORE), min(len(x)/sr, segs[i]["end"] + PAD_AFTER)], cues, vad) for i in bad}
    flat = [w for i in bad for w in wins[i]]
    new = transcribe(x, sr, flat, lang, model_name, prompt, cache, label=label)
    repairs = []; out = list(segs); pos = 0
    for i in bad:
        g = segs[i]; n = len(wins[i]); ks = set(range(pos, pos + n)); pos += n
        cand = [dict(h, region=g["region"]) for h in new if h["region"] in ks and g["start"] - 0.2 <= (h["start"] + h["end"])/2 <= g["end"] + 0.2]
        ok = bool(cand) and skipped(cand, vad) < skipped([g], vad)
        repairs.append((g["start"], g["end"], g["text"].strip(), "".join(h["text"].strip() for h in cand), ok))
        if ok: out[i] = cand
    flat_out = []
    for g in out: flat_out += g if isinstance(g, list) else [g]
    flat_out.sort(key=lambda g: g["start"])
    return flat_out, repairs

*** Hallucination filter
A segment is dropped when Whisper itself flags it (no speech, or a
repetition loop), when it is a known outro phrase, when it echoes the
name prompt (as a whole or by 4-gram overlap), or when the VAD hears
no speech under it and Whisper is unsure there is any (no-speech
probability ≥ =SILENT_NSP=, log-probability ≤ =SILENT_LP=) — the stock
phrases it invents over music or silence — or when it is spoken faster
than the language's =max_rate= allows (from =RATE_MIN_CHARS= characters
on): the same stock phrases squeezed into a fraction of a second after
a real line, where that line's VAD phrase hides them from the silence
test — or when the whole segment is one of the language's =stock=
phrases (ありがとうございました, それではまた, お疲れ様でした …) and
Whisper doubts there is speech (no-speech probability ≥ =STOCK_NSP=)
or stretches a syllable over more than =STOCK_STRETCH= seconds: these
are what Whisper writes over engine noise, panting or a song, and the
VAD may well hear "speech" there.  A real ありがとう scores ≈ 0.4–0.5,
the invented ones ≥ 0.8 (Terminator, 2026-10-17); the no-speech
probability alone does not separate them, a radio voice reaches 0.89.
Real lines the VAD misses
(shouting over music) score well inside those limits.  The loudness
of the audio does not tell the two apart: music is as loud as speech.
#+begin_src python
def strip_punct(t): return re.sub(r"[、。！？!?，,.\s『』「」\"']", "", t)

SILENT_NSP, SILENT_LP = 0.65, -0.6
RATE_MIN_CHARS = 6
STOCK_NSP, STOCK_STRETCH = 0.7, 2.0

def vad_silent(seg, vad):
    """the VAD hears (almost) no speech under the segment, ±0.3 s"""
    s_, e_ = seg["start"] - 0.3, seg["end"] + 0.3
    return sum(max(0.0, min(e_, b) - max(s_, a)) for a, b in vad) <= 0.1*(seg["end"] - seg["start"])

def bad_reason(seg, cfg, prompt, vad=None):
    t = seg["text"].strip()
    if seg["no_speech_prob"] > 0.6 and seg["avg_logprob"] < -1.0: return "no-speech"
    if vad is not None and seg["no_speech_prob"] >= SILENT_NSP and seg["avg_logprob"] <= SILENT_LP and vad_silent(seg, vad): return "silent"
    if seg["compression_ratio"] > 2.4: return "repetition"
    if not t or not re.search(r"\w", t): return "empty"
    n = len(strip_punct(t))
    if n >= RATE_MIN_CHARS and n/max(seg["end"] - seg["start"], 0.01) > cfg["max_rate"]: return "too fast"
    if any(h.lower() in t.lower() for h in cfg["hallu"]): return "hallucination"
    if strip_punct(t).lower() in {strip_punct(s).lower() for s in cfg["stock"]} and (seg["no_speech_prob"] >= STOCK_NSP or
            any(w["end"] - w["start"] > STOCK_STRETCH for w in seg.get("words", []))): return "stock phrase"
    if prompt:
        ts_, ps = strip_punct(t), strip_punct(prompt)
        if len(ts_) >= 6 and ts_ in ps: return "prompt-echo"
        if len(ts_) >= 6 and sum(1 for i in range(len(ts_)-3) if ts_[i:i+4] in ps)/(len(ts_)-3) > 0.7: return "prompt-echo"
    return None
#+end_src
*** Merging the two passes
**** Coverage of VAD speech
How much of the VAD speech inside a region the segments cover.
#+begin_src python
def coverage(segs, rs, re_, vad):
    cov = np.zeros(int((re_-rs)*20)+2)
    for s in segs: cov[max(0, int((s["start"]-rs)*20)):int((s["end"]-rs)*20)+1] = 1
    tot = hit = 0
    for s, e in vad:
        if e <= rs or s >= re_: continue
        s, e = max(s, rs), min(e, re_); n = int((e-s)*20)
        if n <= 0: continue
        tot += n; hit += cov[int((s-rs)*20):int((s-rs)*20)+n].sum()
    return hit/tot if tot else 1.0
#+end_src
**** Merge and name normalisation
For every region the prompted pass wins only when it has no dropped
segment and covers the VAD speech at least as well as the unprompted
pass; otherwise the unprompted pass is used.  Then the name variants
are replaced by their canonical spelling.
#+begin_src python
def merge_passes(A, B, regions, vad, cfg, prompt, names):
    out = []; used = dict(prompt=0, noprompt=0); dropped = []
    for k, (rs, re_) in enumerate(regions):
        a = [s for s in (A or []) if s["region"] == k]; b = [s for s in B if s["region"] == k]
        a_ok = [s for s in a if not bad_reason(s, cfg, prompt, vad)]
        b_ok = [s for s in b if not bad_reason(s, cfg, None, vad)]
        dropped += [(s, bad_reason(s, cfg, prompt, vad)) for s in a if bad_reason(s, cfg, prompt, vad)]
        dropped += [(s, bad_reason(s, cfg, None, vad)) for s in b if bad_reason(s, cfg, None, vad)]
        if A is not None and len(a_ok) == len(a) and coverage(a_ok, rs, re_, vad) >= coverage(b_ok, rs, re_, vad) - 0.05:
            out += a_ok; used["prompt"] += 1
        else:
            out += b_ok; used["noprompt"] += 1
    out.sort(key=lambda s: s["start"])
    normalise_names(out, names)
    return out, used, dropped

def normalise_names(segs, names):
    for canon, variants in names.items():
        for v in variants:
            for s in segs:
                if v in s["text"]:
                    s["text"] = s["text"].replace(v, canon)
                    for w in s["words"]: w["w"] = w["w"].replace(v, canon)

**** Corrections file
Mishearings a person has confirmed, one per line: =wrong<TAB>right=,
optionally a third column =H:MM:SS= that limits the fix to the Whisper
segment holding that time; =#= starts a comment.  The file lives
outside the work dir — =/tmp/subtitles= does not survive a reboot, and
a correction is the one thing here a person made — by default at
=~/.local/share/subtitles/fixes/<basename>.tsv=, read when present.

=-n= cannot do this: it rewrites each Whisper token on its own, and
Whisper's Japanese tokens are sub-word pieces, so a mishearing spread
over several of them (過|労|死) was never matched — and names also feed
the prompt, which changes the transcription itself.  Here the
replacement is made across tokens: it goes on the first token touched,

def read_fixes(path):
    """-> [(wrong, right, time or None)]"""
    out = []
    if not path or not os.path.exists(path): return out
    for ln in open(path, encoding="utf-8"):
        if not ln.strip() or ln.lstrip().startswith("#"): continue
        f = ln.rstrip("\n").split("\t")
        if len(f) < 2 or not f[0]: continue
        at = None
        if len(f) > 2 and f[2].strip():
            p = [float(v) for v in f[2].strip().split(":")]
            at = sum(v * 60**(len(p) - 1 - i) for i, v in enumerate(p))
        out.append((f[0], f[1], at))
    return out

def apply_fixes(segs, fixes):
    """-> [((wrong, right, time), times applied)]"""
    done = []
    for wrong, right, at in fixes:
        n = 0
        for s in segs:
            if at is not None and not (s["start"] - 1 <= at <= s["end"] + 1): continue
            k0, here = 0, 0
            while True:
                ws = s["words"]; joined = "".join(w["w"] for w in ws)
                k = joined.find(wrong, k0)
                if k < 0: break
                pos = 0; first = last = None; fo = lo = 0
                for i, w in enumerate(ws):
                    a, b = pos, pos + len(w["w"])
                    if first is None and b > k: first, fo = i, a
                    if a < k + len(wrong): last, lo = i, a
                    pos = b
                head, tail = ws[first]["w"][:k - fo], ws[last]["w"][k + len(wrong) - lo:]
                ws[first]["w"] = head + right + tail; ws[first]["end"] = ws[last]["end"]
                del ws[first + 1:last + 1]
                k0 = k + len(right); here += 1
            if here: s["text"] = "".join(w["w"] for w in s["words"]); n += here
        done.append(((wrong, right, at), n))
    return done

*** Speakers
**** Phrases
The unit of voice identity is a stretch of speech between silences of
≥ =PHRASE_GAP= — found by a fine VAD pass over the audio, not by
Whisper's word gaps, which inside a segment are absorbed into the
neighbouring words.  Each token joins the phrase it overlaps most (a
tolerance of =EDGE_TOL= for Whisper's early lean), in time order and
never an earlier phrase than the token before it.  A token overlapping
no phrase joins the nearest phrased token of its own Whisper segment
(Whisper often stretches a segment's first token back into the silence
before it); with none in its segment it stays without a phrase —
never the previous line's phrase across a silence, which would make
two lines far apart look like one stretch of speech.

**** Clustering: long phrases define the voices
Embeddings of phrases shorter than a second are unreliable, so only
phrases ≥ =SPK_LONG= are clustered (average linkage, cosine distance,
cut at =SPK_DIST=).  Every shorter phrase then joins the cluster whose
centroid is nearest among the voices heard within =SPK_NEAR= seconds
of it — a handful of candidates instead of the whole cast.

def cluster_voices(phr, E):
    from scipy.cluster.hierarchy import linkage, fcluster
    n = len(phr); lab = [0]*n
    longi = [i for i, p_ in enumerate(phr) if p_["end"] - p_["start"] >= SPK_LONG]
    if len(longi) < 2:
        return [1]*n
    cl = fcluster(linkage(E[longi], method="average", metric="cosine"), t=SPK_DIST, criterion="distance")
    for i, c in zip(longi, cl): lab[i] = int(c)
    cent = {}
    for c in set(cl):
        v = E[[i for i, cc in zip(longi, cl) if cc == c]].mean(axis=0); cent[int(c)] = v/(np.linalg.norm(v)+1e-9)
    for i, p_ in enumerate(phr):
        if lab[i]: continue
        near = {lab[j] for j in longi if abs(phr[j]["start"] - p_["start"]) <= SPK_NEAR} or set(cent)
        lab[i] = max(near, key=lambda c: float(E[i] @ cent[c]))
    return lab

**** Robust labels
Whisper's word times are ±0.3 s.  A change of label between two runs
of words is therefore placed at the best legal token boundary near the
audio silence — legal: the two tokens are ≥ =PHRASE_GAP= apart or a
VAD/cue edge lies within =EDGE_TOL= of them, the token after it does
not begin with a small kana or ー, and the boundary is not inside a
katakana or kanji compound — searching two tokens either side; without
one, the change is dropped.  A run is also dropped (absorbed by the
neighbour nearer in time) when it is shorter than =RUN_MIN_WORDS= or
=RUN_MIN_SEC=, or when its own voice embedding is not clearly different
from the previous run's (cosine similarity ≥ =SPK_CONTRAST=) — cluster
ids alone over-split a whole film.  A word in no phrase has no voice
and no label; it takes no part.

def is_kata(ch): return "\u30a0" <= ch <= "\u30ff"
def is_kanji(ch): return "\u4e00" <= ch <= "\u9fff"

def legal_boundary(words, j, bounds):
    """may a speaker's line start at words[j]?"""
    if j <= 0 or j >= len(words): return False
    a, b = words[j-1], words[j]
    ta, tb = a["w"].strip(), b["w"].strip()
    if not ta or not tb or tb[:1] in NO_LINE_START: return False
    if (is_kata(ta[-1]) and is_kata(tb[0])) or (is_kanji(ta[-1]) and is_kanji(tb[0])): return False   # inside a word
    if b["start"] - a["end"] >= PHRASE_GAP: return True
    i = bisect.bisect_left(bounds, a["end"] - EDGE_TOL)
    return i < len(bounds) and bounds[i] <= b["start"] + EDGE_TOL

*** Word boundaries
Whisper's Japanese "words" are sub-word pieces (お|前, 頼|む), so a
split or a line break needs real word boundaries: MeCab (fugashi with
unidic-lite) segments the text and tags parts of speech.  A boundary
is allowed at a word start that begins a new bunsetsu — not before a
particle, an auxiliary, a suffix, or a verb attached by て/で — and
scores a bonus after a particle, an auxiliary or punctuation.  Without

ATTACH_NEXT = {"助詞", "助動詞", "接尾辞"}                       # a word of this kind hangs on the previous one
FORMAL_NOUNS = {"限り", "こと", "もの", "とき", "時", "ため", "はず", "わけ", "つもり", "ところ", "うち", "せい", "よう", "そう", "まま", "ほう", "方"}

def ja_boundaries(text):
    """-> {char offset: bonus} for every allowed boundary inside text (a bonus < 0 marks a bunsetsu end)"""
    tg = tagger()
    if tg is None: return None
    words = [(w.surface, w.feature.pos1) for w in tg(text)]
    out = {}; off = 0; prev = None
    for surf, pos in words:
        if prev is not None and off > 0:
            ps, pp = prev
            ok = pos not in ATTACH_NEXT and pos in HEAD_POS and pp != "接頭辞"
            if ok and pos == "動詞" and pp == "助詞" and ps in ("て", "で"): ok = False
            if ok and pos == "名詞" and surf in FORMAL_NOUNS and pp in ("動詞", "形容詞", "助動詞", "形状詞"): ok = False
            if ok:
                bonus = -0.4 if pp in ("助詞", "助動詞") or pp in ("補助記号", "記号") else (-0.2 if pp in ("動詞", "形容詞") else 0.0)
                out[off] = bonus
        off += len(surf); prev = (surf, pos)
    return out

    adjective or auxiliary in a final form with no particle or auxiliary after it — and after a clause-final one —
    a conjunctive particle (ば, が, ながら, て), or a verb or adjective in its 連用形 followed by a new word (基づき|ファリス)"""
    tg = tagger()

        elif f.pos2 == "接続助詞" and not attached: cl.add(off)

def break_lines(t, cfg):

**** Timing and assembly

def clean_text(ws, cfg):
    text = "".join(w["w"] for w in ws).strip()
    text = re.sub(r"\s+", " " if cfg["spaces"] else "", text)
    if cfg["strip_end"]: text = re.sub("[" + re.escape(cfg["strip_end"]) + "]+$", "", text)
    return text

def speaker_runs(ws):
    """consecutive words of one speaker -> list of word lists (one list when unlabelled)"""
    runs = []
    for w in ws:
        if runs and runs[-1][0].get("spk") == w.get("spk"): runs[-1].append(w)
        else: runs.append([w])
    return runs

def timed(ws, starts, ends):
    st, en = max(0.0, ws[0]["start"]-LEAD), ws[-1]["end"]+LINGER
    s2, e2 = nearest(ws[0]["start"], starts), nearest(ws[-1]["end"], ends)
    if s2 is not None and s2 <= ws[0]["start"]+0.05: st = s2
    if e2 is not None and e2 >= ws[-1]["end"]-0.05: en = e2
    if en-st < MIN_DUR: en = st + MIN_DUR
    return st, en

def unit_runs(ws):
    """speaker runs inside one cue; a run too short to be a line of its own joins its neighbour"""
    runs = speaker_runs(ws)
    while len(runs) > 1:
        short = [k for k, r in enumerate(runs) if len(r) < RUN_MIN_WORDS or r[-1]["end"] - r[0]["start"] < RUN_MIN_SEC]
        if not short: break
        k = short[0]; tgt = k-1 if k else 1
        runs[min(k, tgt)] = runs[min(k, tgt)] + runs[max(k, tgt)]; del runs[max(k, tgt)]
    return runs

    out.sort(key=lambda c: c["start"])
    merged = []
    for c in out:
        if merged and not c["multi"] and not merged[-1]["multi"] and len(c["text"]) <= 3 and c["start"]-merged[-1]["end"] < 0.4 and len(merged[-1]["text"])+len(c["text"]) <= 2*cfg["maxline"]:
            merged[-1]["text"] += ("" if not cfg["spaces"] else " ") + c["text"]; merged[-1]["end"] = max(merged[-1]["end"], c["end"]); continue
        merged.append(c)
    for a, b in zip(merged, merged[1:]):
        if a["end"] > b["start"]-GAP: a["end"] = max(a["start"]+0.5, b["start"]-GAP)
    return [(c["start"], c["end"], c["text"] if c["multi"] else break_lines(c["text"], cfg), c["logprob"], c["multi"]) for c in merged]

*** Reference-driven assembly
**** Apportioning words to the reference cues
For every boundary between two consecutive reference cues, the words
are split at the best legal token boundary within ±=APPORTION_WIN= s of
the reference edge: closeness in time, with bonuses for a real silence
between the tokens (the fine VAD phrases — Whisper compresses pauses
into the neighbouring tokens), for sentence punctuation and for a
pause; when no legal boundary lies within the window the search
widens to twice it, and only then is an illegal position accepted.
Between two Whisper segments every position is legal (unless the next
token may not begin a line): the kanji|kanji rule is for the inside of
a word, and no word spans two segments — 緊急 | 毛玉 was refused there.
A cue whose next word already lies past its edge gets no words —
the search starts after the first word and would otherwise hand it
one (どう|したんだ, once an invented line no longer filled that cue).
Whole phrases far from any cue (≥ =OUTSIDE_GAP= s beyond the cue's
edges, detached at a real gap of ≥ 0.5 s) are left for the free
assembly.
#+begin_src python
APPORTION_WIN, OUTSIDE_GAP = 1.0, 1.5

def illegal_chars(a, b):
    ta, tb = a["w"].strip(), b["w"].strip()
    return (not tb or tb[:1] in NO_LINE_START or (ta and ((is_kata(ta[-1]) and is_kata(tb[0])) or (is_kanji(ta[-1]) and is_kanji(tb[0])))))

def illegal(a, b):
    if "wb" in b and a.get("seg") == b.get("seg"): return b["wb"] is None
    if a.get("seg") is not None and b.get("seg") is not None and a["seg"] != b["seg"]:     # two Whisper segments: no word spans them
        tb = b["w"].strip(); return not tb or tb[:1] in NO_LINE_START
    return illegal_chars(a, b)

def boundary_score(words, j, B, cfg):
    """lower is better: distance from the wanted time, minus bonuses for a real silence, sentence punctuation, a pause"""
    a, b = words[j-1], words[j]
    t = (a["end"] + b["start"])/2; sc = abs(t - B)
    if a.get("phr") is not None and a.get("phr") != b.get("phr"): sc -= 0.6      # a VAD silence lies between the tokens
    if b.get("wb"): sc += b["wb"]                                                  # bunsetsu end before it
    if re.search("[" + re.escape(cfg["punct_end"]) + "]$", a["w"].strip()): sc -= 0.5
    if b["start"] - a["end"] >= 0.3: sc -= 0.3
    if illegal(a, b): sc += 3.0
    return sc

def apportion(words, cues, cfg):
    """-> list of word lists, one per reference cue (may be empty), plus the words outside all cues"""
    n = len(words); groups = [[] for _ in cues]; outside = []; k = 0
    for i, (s_, e_, _) in enumerate(cues):
        nxt = cues[i+1][0] if i+1 < len(cues) else None
        B = (e_ + nxt)/2 if nxt is not None and nxt > e_ else (e_ if nxt is None else e_)
        # words clearly before this cue that were not taken by the previous one — whole phrases only (a real gap follows)
        while k < n and words[k]["end"] < s_ - OUTSIDE_GAP and k+1 < n and words[k+1]["start"] - words[k]["end"] >= 0.5 and not illegal(words[k], words[k+1]):
            outside.append(words[k]); k += 1
        while k < n and words[k]["end"] < s_ - OUTSIDE_GAP:
            j = k+1
            while j < n and not (words[j]["start"] - words[j-1]["end"] >= 0.5 and not illegal(words[j-1], words[j])): j += 1
            if j < n and words[j-1]["end"] < s_ - OUTSIDE_GAP: outside += words[k:j]; k = j
            else: break
        if nxt is None: end = n
        elif k < n and (words[k]["start"] + words[k]["end"])/2 >= B: end = k      # the next word is past the edge: nothing for this cue
        else:
            near = lambda win: [j for j in range(k+1, n) if abs((words[j-1]["end"] + words[j]["start"])/2 - B) <= win]
            legal = [j for j in near(APPORTION_WIN) if not illegal(words[j-1], words[j])] or [j for j in near(2*APPORTION_WIN) if not illegal(words[j-1], words[j])]
            cand = legal or near(APPORTION_WIN)
            if cand: end = min(cand, key=lambda j: boundary_score(words, j, B, cfg))
            else:
                # no token boundary near the reference edge: split where the words are, on its side of the edge
                end = k
                while end < n and (words[end]["start"] + words[end]["end"])/2 < B: end += 1
        g = words[k:end]; k = end
        # words far past this cue's end belong to nobody (speech without a reference cue) — whole phrases only
        while g:
            j = len(g) - 1
            while j > 0 and not (g[j]["start"] - g[j-1]["end"] >= 0.5 and not illegal(g[j-1], g[j])): j -= 1
            if j > 0 and g[j]["start"] > e_ + OUTSIDE_GAP: outside += g[j:]; del g[j:]
            else: break
        groups[i] = g
    outside += words[k:]
    outside.sort(key=lambda w: w["start"])
    return groups, outside

**** Lines inside a reference cue
Two sentences or a turn inside one cue go on two lines, as on the
disc: a line ends at sentence punctuation followed by a pause, or at a
pause ≥ =TURN_GAP= s, or at a Whisper segment edge with a pause —
failing all of these, at a Whisper segment edge without one: a reply
often follows with no pause at all (レッツィー奥で問題があったの /
問題?, Terminator 3), and Whisper's new segment is the only trace of
the turn.  A change of voice (=-d on=, the default) comes before all
of them: where the speaker labels change inside the cue, the lines
part.  Each kind is tried in that order, and the first whose two lines
both fit wins: a sentence end with a pause that would leave a line too
long must not hide the pause-less turn just before it (レッツィー奥で
問題があったの / 問題?こっちの方が緊急).
A change of voice or a clear turn where no break leaves two lines that
fit makes the text two cues, cut at a sentence boundary: broken by
length, the turn would land inside a line and the reply run on from the
question (まっさらで自分が育った / 大昔を思い出せるかかすかに,
Mission: Impossible III, 33:00 — three utterances, two speakers).
Where a two-speaker cue of the check track lies over the words, a bare
Whisper segment edge counts as such a turn too: the reply often follows
the question with no pause, and the new segment is its only trace
(ベンジーで読めたのかああそりゃ今 / 述べたように最悪の状態ではあるが,
33:31 — -Benji. Did you recover anything? / -Yes. Yes.).  Without that
evidence a pause-less segment edge is as often one speaker going on, and
the text is not cut there.
Without a turn the two lines part at a sentence end, else at a clause end
(基づき / ファリスの任務参加を認めた), when both fit; only then is the
text broken by length.  More than two lines' worth
becomes two cues, split at the best boundary.
#+begin_src python
TURN_GAP = 0.25

def turn_tiers(ws, cfg):
    """candidate line breaks in order of preference: a change of voice, a clear turn, a bare Whisper segment edge"""
    voice = []; pts = []; weak = []
    for j in range(1, len(ws)):
        a, b = ws[j-1], ws[j]; gap = b["start"] - a["end"]
        punct = bool(re.search("[" + re.escape(cfg["punct_end"]) + "]$", a["w"].strip()))
        newseg = a.get("seg") is not None and a.get("seg") != b.get("seg")
        silence = a.get("phr") is not None and a.get("phr") != b.get("phr")
        if illegal(a, b): continue
        if a.get("spk") is not None and b.get("spk") is not None and a["spk"] != b["spk"]: voice.append(j)
        if (punct and (gap >= 0.08 or silence)) or gap >= TURN_GAP or (newseg and (gap >= 0.12 or silence)): pts.append(j)
        elif newseg: weak.append(j)
    return [voice, pts, weak]

def turn_points(ws, cfg):
    return next((tier for tier in turn_tiers(ws, cfg) if tier), [])

def dialogue_over(ws, check):
    """a two-speaker cue of the check track lies over the words"""
    a, b = ws[0]["start"], ws[-1]["end"]
    return any(d and min(e, b) - max(s, a) > 0.5*min(e - s, b - a) for s, e, d in check)

def cue_text(ws, cfg, strict=True, check=()):
    """-> list of lines (1 or 2), or None when the text needs two cues — strict: also when a turn cannot be the break"""
    n = cfg["maxline"]; tiers = turn_tiers(ws, cfg)
    full = clean_text(ws, cfg)
    if len(full) <= n and not any(tiers): return [full]
    for tier in tiers:
        # the most balanced break of the most preferred kind whose two lines both fit
        fit = [(abs(len(a) - len(b)), [a, b]) for j in tier for a, b in [(clean_text(ws[:j], cfg), clean_text(ws[j:], cfg))] if len(a) <= n and len(b) <= n]
        if fit: return min(fit, key=lambda f: f[0])[1]
    if strict and (tiers[0] or tiers[1] or (tiers[2] and dialogue_over(ws, check))): return None   # a break by length would bury the turn
    if len(full) <= 2*n:
        # no turn: a sentence or clause end whose two lines both fit, before a break by length alone
        for kind in ("se", "cl"):
            fit = [(abs(len(a) - len(b)), [a, b]) for j in range(1, len(ws)) if ws[j-1].get(kind) and not illegal(ws[j-1], ws[j])
                   for a, b in [(clean_text(ws[:j], cfg), clean_text(ws[j:], cfg))] if len(a) <= n and len(b) <= n and min(len(a), len(b)) >= 3]
            if fit: return min(fit, key=lambda f: f[0])[1]
        return break_lines(full, cfg).split("\n")
    return None

def split_cue(ws, cfg):
    """best token boundary to divide an overlong cue in two"""
    mid = (ws[0]["start"] + ws[-1]["end"])/2
    return min(range(1, len(ws)), key=lambda j: boundary_score(ws, j, mid, cfg))

**** Whole sentences across reference cues
The reference's division into cues serves its own language: an English
track cuts a long sentence into cues of ≈ 2 s for reading, and the
dub says the same passage as one sentence — apportioned cue by cue it
came out in pieces cut at the English edges (私は君の評価に基づき |
ファリスの任務参加を認めた, Mission: Impossible III, 28:05–28:16: two
sentences in five cues).  So consecutive reference groups (≤
=JOIN_CUE_GAP= apart) are chained wherever the words across their
edge lie inside one sentence — no sentence end (punctuation or, for
Japanese, a final form or final particle), no Whisper segment edge, no
pause of =TURN_GAP= or a VAD silence of =SENT_PAUSE=, no change of
voice.  A chain is one cue when it fits — two lines that each fit, =MAX_DUR=
on screen —
else it is cut where it must be, the best kind of cut first: a
sentence end, then a clause end (、, ば, が, ながら, a 連用形 such as
基づき), then the end of a bunsetsu, then any legal boundary; the most
balanced of that kind, and again on each part until every part
fits.  A cue keeps its reference start and end as a floor only where
it begins or ends a chain; a part cut out of a chain takes its words'
own edges.
#+begin_src python
SENT_PAUSE, JOIN_CUE_GAP = 0.35, 1.0

def sentence_break(a, b, cfg, phrases):
    """the words a | b do not lie inside one sentence"""
    if a.get("se") or re.search("[" + re.escape(cfg["punct_end"]) + "]$", a["w"].strip()): return True
    if a.get("seg") is not None and b.get("seg") is not None and a["seg"] != b["seg"]: return True
    if b["start"] - a["end"] >= TURN_GAP: return True
    if a.get("spk") is not None and b.get("spk") is not None and a["spk"] != b["spk"]: return True
    if phrases and a.get("phr") is not None and b.get("phr") is not None and a["phr"] != b["phr"]:
        if phrases[b["phr"]]["start"] - phrases[a["phr"]]["end"] >= SENT_PAUSE: return True
    return False

def fits(ws, cfg, check=()):
    """one cue: on screen at most MAX_DUR with its lead-in and linger, two lines that each fit"""
    if ws[-1]["end"] - ws[0]["start"] > MAX_DUR - LEAD - LINGER or len(clean_text(ws, cfg)) > 2*cfg["maxline"]: return False
    lines = cue_text(ws, cfg, check=check)
    return lines is not None and all(len(l) <= cfg["maxline"] for l in lines)

def cut_kind(ws, j, cfg, phrases):
    a, b = ws[j-1], ws[j]
    if illegal(a, b): return 9
    if sentence_break(a, b, cfg, phrases): return 0
    if a.get("cl"): return 1
    if (b.get("wb") or 0) < 0: return 2
    return 3

def split_sentence(ws, cfg, phrases, check=()):
    """a chain of words -> parts that each fit one cue, cut at the best kind of boundary, most balanced first"""
    if len(ws) < 2 or fits(ws, cfg, check): return [ws]
    def key(j):
        la, lb = len(clean_text(ws[:j], cfg)), len(clean_text(ws[j:], cfg))
        kind = cut_kind(ws, j, cfg, phrases)
        return (kind + (2 if kind and min(la, lb) < 4 else 0), abs(la - lb))
    j = min(range(1, len(ws)), key=key)
    return split_sentence(ws[:j], cfg, phrases, check) + split_sentence(ws[j:], cfg, phrases, check)

def sentence_groups(groups, cues, cfg, phrases, check=()):
    """-> (cue windows, word groups): reference groups whose shared edge lies inside a sentence joined, each chain cut where it must"""
    chains = []
    for i, g in enumerate(groups):
        if not g: continue
        if chains and cues[i][0] - cues[chains[-1][1][-1]][1] <= JOIN_CUE_GAP and not sentence_break(chains[-1][0][-1], g[0], cfg, phrases):
            chains[-1][0].extend(g); chains[-1][1].append(i)
        else: chains.append([list(g), [i]])
    win, out = [], []
    for ws, idx in chains:
        if len(idx) == 1: win.append(cues[idx[0]]); out.append(ws); continue
        parts = split_sentence(ws, cfg, phrases, check)
        for k, p_ in enumerate(parts):
            win.append((cues[idx[0]][0] if k == 0 else p_[0]["start"], cues[idx[-1]][1] if k == len(parts)-1 else p_[-1]["end"], ""))
            out.append(p_)
    return win, out

**** Two speakers in one cue
A cue whose two lines are two speakers marks each line with the
language's =dash= — for Japanese the half-width hyphen and a space
(=- まさか= / =- もちろん=), as in Netflix's Japanese style guide; a
long-vowel ー at a line's start would read as part of the word.  The lines must part
at a turn — not at a break by length — and then one of three things
must say that the voice changes there:
- the speaker labels of the two lines differ;
- a two-speaker cue of the check track lies over the cue's speech —
  the original's subtitles begin each speaker's line with a dash
  (=-I am, sir.= / =-I'm not on you yet.=);
- the two lines lie under two different cues of the check track and
  their own voices differ: an ECAPA embedding of each line's audio
  (Whisper's word span corrected by its lean, at least 1 s) with a
  cosine similarity below =SPK_CONTRAST=.  The dub lags the original,
  so the end of one speaker's sentence lands in the next speaker's
  cue (手下を捉えても何の情報も / 本件の責任者は君か, Mission:
  Impossible III, 28:24).  Neither of the other two sees it: over a
  whole film the clustering often puts a scene's voices into one
  cluster, and with no silence between the speakers both lines share
  one VAD phrase and its embedding.  On the 37 such cues of that film
  20 of 25 real turns fall below the cut and 8 of 12 lines of one
  speaker stay above it — the check cue alone would have dashed all 37.
#+begin_src python
def dialogue_cue(text):
    """a subtitle cue holding two speakers: a dash opens a line, or follows a sentence end inside one, at least twice — or
    once where it is not the cue's very start (the second speaker only)"""
    t = re.sub(r"</?[^>]+>|\{[^}]*\}", "", text).strip()
    marks = [m.start() for m in re.finditer(r"(?:^|\n|(?<=[.!?…\"'♪])\s+)\s*[-–—]\s*(?=\S)", t)]
    return len(marks) >= 2 or (len(marks) == 1 and marks[0] > 0)

_ENC = None
def line_voice(x, sr, a, b):
    """ECAPA embedding of the audio a..b (at least 1 s, centred)"""
    global _ENC
    import torch
    if _ENC is None:
        from speechbrain.inference.speaker import EncoderClassifier
        _ENC = EncoderClassifier.from_hparams(source="speechbrain/spkrec-ecapa-voxceleb", savedir=os.path.join(MODELS_DIR, "ecapa"), run_opts={"device": "cuda:0"})
    if b - a < 1.0: m = (a + b)/2; a, b = m - 0.5, m + 0.5
    a0, b0 = max(0, int(a*sr)), min(len(x), int(b*sr))
    with torch.no_grad(): e = _ENC.encode_batch(torch.from_numpy(x[a0:b0]).unsqueeze(0).to("cuda:0")).squeeze().cpu().numpy()
    return e/(np.linalg.norm(e) + 1e-9)

def two_speakers(g, lines, cfg, check, sp_st, sp_en, x=None, sr=None, lean=0.2):
    """the two lines of a cue are two speakers: they part at a turn, and the voices differ, or the check track has a
    two-speaker cue here, or the lines lie under two check cues and their own voices differ"""
    j = next((j for j in range(1, len(g)) if clean_text(g[:j], cfg) == lines[0]), None)
    if j is None: return False
    voice, pts, weak = turn_tiers(g, cfg)
    if j in voice: return True
    if j not in pts and j not in weak: return False
    for s, e, dlg in check:
        if dlg and min(e, sp_en) - max(s, sp_st) > 0.5*min(e - s, sp_en - sp_st): return True
    if x is None: return False
    def under(ws):
        m = float(np.median([(w["start"] + w["end"])/2 for w in ws]))
        return next((k for k, (s, e, _) in enumerate(check) if s <= m <= e + 0.3), None)
    ka, kb = under(g[:j]), under(g[j:])
    if ka is None or kb is None or ka == kb: return False
    va = line_voice(x, sr, g[0]["start"] + lean, g[j-1]["end"] + lean); vb = line_voice(x, sr, g[j]["start"] + lean, g[-1]["end"] + lean)
    return float(va @ vb) < SPK_CONTRAST

**** Speech edges of a group of words
The VAD phrases give the real edges of the speech: a cue's speech
starts where the phrase holding its first word starts and ends where
the phrase holding its last word ends — unless that phrase is shared
with the neighbouring cue (no silence between the lines), in which
case the edge is the midpoint between the two cues' adjacent words,
corrected by Whisper's lean — its word times run early by a fairly
constant amount, measured on the film itself against the VAD edges
(≈ 0.2 s).
#+begin_src python
def whisper_lean(phrases):
    """how early Whisper's word times run against the VAD edges on this film (median over phrases >= 0.6 s)"""
    ends = [p_["end"] - p_["words"][-1]["end"] for p_ in phrases.values() if p_["end"] - p_["start"] >= 0.6]
    starts = [p_["start"] - p_["words"][0]["start"] for p_ in phrases.values() if p_["end"] - p_["start"] >= 0.6]
    if len(ends) < 20: return 0.2
    return float(max(0.0, min(0.5, (np.median(ends) + np.median(starts))/2)))

def speech_edges(g, phrases, prev_last=None, next_first=None, lean=0.2):
    """speech start and end of a group of words: the VAD phrase edges, or — when the neighbouring cue shares the
    phrase (no pause between the lines) — the midpoint of Whisper's times corrected by its measured lean"""
    a, b = g[0], g[-1]
    st, en = a["start"], b["end"] + lean
    if phrases and a.get("phr") is not None:
        pa = phrases[a["phr"]]
        st = (prev_last["end"] + a["start"])/2 + lean if prev_last is not None and prev_last.get("phr") == a["phr"] else pa["start"]
    if phrases and b.get("phr") is not None:
        pb = phrases[b["phr"]]
        en = (b["end"] + next_first["start"])/2 + lean if next_first is not None and next_first.get("phr") == b["phr"] else max(pb["end"], b["end"])
    return st, en

**** Timing and output
A cue starts at its speech start — the reference start only when no
VAD phrase holds its first word, since the reference often runs ahead
of the dub — and ends =LINGER= after its speech ends (the reference end
as a floor).  When neighbours collide, the earlier cue keeps at least
its speech and the later one starts after it — the reference start of
a following line never trims a line that is still being spoken; only
when the speech itself overlaps is the boundary the midpoint.

def conf(ws):
    """mean word probability of a cue (0..1)"""
    p = [w.get("p", 1.0) for w in ws]; return sum(p)/len(p) if p else 1.0

*** Whole sentences, one per cue
=-g sentence=, the default.  The assembly above still answers to the
reference: it chains cues only while the chain fits two lines and
=MAX_DUR=, and cuts a longer sentence where it must — so a long line of
the dub came out in pieces, sometimes inside a word (Terminator 2,
1:58: …呼びましたが、その | 後もっと過酷な運命が…).  For study, and for
shiroikuma-jisho's player, which stops after every cue, a cue must be
one whole sentence.

So the cues the assembly wrote are regrouped.  Their text and their
speech-snapped edges are kept; only where a cue ends changes.  The
cues' characters are aligned with Whisper's words (=SequenceMatcher=,
=autojunk= off), and a sentence ends after a character whose word
closes a sentence — punctuation, a final form or final particle from
MeCab (=se=), or the end of a Whisper segment — at the end of a line of
two speakers (a turn), at the cue's own =?= or =!=, and wherever two
cues are more than =SENT_GAP= apart.  A sentence that begins or ends
where a cue did keeps that edge; one that ends inside a cue takes its
last word's end plus =SENT_TAIL=, clamped to the cue.  A cue is then as
many lines of =sentline= characters as the sentence needs, broken at
MeCab bunsetsu boundaries, after punctuation first.  =-g cue= keeps
the reference-sized cues.

        if fin: break
        lines += 1
    if not fin: return "\n".join(t[k:k + n] for k in range(0, len(t), n))
    e = [0] + min(fin)[1] + [len(t)]
    return "\n".join(t[a:b].strip() for a, b in zip(e, e[1:]))

*** Cross-check against the original's subtitles
Whisper writes what a word sounds like, and a dub is full of words that
sound like other words: Terminator, 1:26–1:31, Sarah calls カイル and the
track read 帰る four times; 25:43 次のサラ・コナー came out 次の皿コーナー;
Terminator 2, 1:54, 核の炎で — "the nuclear fire" — came out 過労死で.
Each is plain nonsense next to the original's line, and each sounds
almost exactly like the right one.  So the original's subtitles — the
check track, English on these discs — are read for what the line says,
and the audio decides every correction.  Japanese only (readings come
from MeCab); for other languages the stage is skipped.

1. *Names.*  Proper nouns of the original — capitalised away from a
   sentence start, at least twice, never in lower case — are paired with
   the dub's katakana words heard under the same lines, when the two
   sound alike (consonant skeletons, r = l, vowels dropped: Kyle =
   カイル = k-r).  The dub's own spelling is learnt from the lines
   Whisper got right.
2. *A missing name.*  When the original's line names someone and the
   Japanese line lacks that name, every span of one to three MeCab
   words is read in kana and compared with the name; a span that sounds
   like it (帰る カエル for カイル, 皿 サラ for サラ) is a candidate.
3. *Meaning.*  LaBSE, a cross-language sentence embedding, scores each
   line against the original's lines under it.  A dub paraphrases, so a
   low score only makes the line a suspect; so does a number of two or
   more digits in the original that the line lacks.
4. *Candidates from the audio.*  A suspect's window is decoded again,
   with the film's names as the prompt and without, and a reading near
   enough to the line (no neighbour's speech pulled in) is a candidate.
5. *The audio decides.*  Every candidate and the current line are scored
   by forced decoding: Whisper's log-probability of exactly that text for
   exactly that audio, with the names as context — the same prior for
   both, so the film's names are not penalised for being foreign.  A
   name span is taken when it scores at least =XC_NAME_MARGIN= against
   the current text; a re-decoded line only when it scores better by
   =XC_MARGIN= *and* brings in the name or the number the original has
   and the line lacks, losing no more than a character of the line on
   the way.

   Nothing weaker is taken, and both weaker rules were tried.  A LaBSE
   gain is noise at this length: on Terminator 2 a change of punctuation
   alone gained +0.17 and 大式 → 大好き +0.13, while the genuine 頭部を測って
   → 逃亡を図って gained +0.04.  Nor is an audio far surer of the new text
   enough: 鎌は → ママは (Δ +9.61), 来て → していけないんだに (Δ +5.02) and a
   ダイソーさん the names prompt put into a line (Δ +8.18) all passed that
   way.  Every acceptance on Terminator 1 and 2 that was right brought a
   name or a number; the rest stay listed as candidates to listen to.

Everything taken is listed in the review, before and after, with the
scores; suspects nothing resolved are listed apart, with the original's
line, for a person to listen to — and a confirmed fix goes into the
corrections file.  Results are cached per line in the work dir.

def skeleton(t):
    """a word's consonant skeleton, katakana or Latin: Kyle = カイル = kr, Sarah = サラ = sr, Connor = コナー = kn"""
    if re.search("[ァ-ヴ]", t):
        t = "".join(KANA.get(c, "") for c in kata(t))
    t = t.lower()
    for a, b in (("sh", "s"), ("ch", "c"), ("ts", "t"), ("ph", "f"), ("ck", "k"), ("qu", "k"), ("x", "ks"), ("th", "s")):
        t = t.replace(a, b)
    t = re.sub(r"c(?=[eiy])", "s", t).replace("c", "k").replace("q", "k").replace("l", "r").replace("v", "b").replace("j", "z")
    t = re.sub(r"[^a-z]", "", t); t = re.sub(r"[aeiouyhw]", "", t)
    return re.sub(r"(.)\1+", r"\1", t)

def sound_sim(a, b):

    sa, sb = skeleton(a), skeleton(b)

def name_map(cues, check, names):
    """{English name: the dub's katakana for it}, learnt from the lines that carry both"""
    pairs = []
    for nm in names:
        co = {}
        for c in cues:
            if not any(re.search(r"\b%s\b" % nm, e) for e in english_under(c, check)): continue
            for k in set(re.findall(KATA_RE, c["text"].replace("・", " "))): co[k] = co.get(k, 0) + 1
        pairs += [(sound_sim(nm, k), n, nm, k) for k, n in co.items() if n >= 2 and sound_sim(nm, k) >= 0.6]
    # one katakana word, one name: the best-sounding pair wins it (Broadway, not Ford, gets ブロードウェイ; Connor, not
    # Katherine, gets コナー) — shared only between variants of one name (Kate/Katie, Terminator/Terminators)
    out = {}
    for sim, n, nm, k in sorted(pairs, reverse=True):
        if nm in out: continue
        if any(v == k and sound_sim(o, nm) < 0.8 for o, v in out.items()): continue
        out[nm] = k
    return out

def name_spans(text, k):
    """-> [(similarity, surface)] spans of one to three MeCab words that sound like the katakana k"""
    tg = tagger()
    if tg is None: return []
    toks = [(w.surface, kata(w.feature.kana if getattr(w.feature, "kana", None) not in (None, "*") else w.surface)) for w in tg(text)]
    out = []
    for i in range(len(toks)):

def halves(c, words):
    """-> the cut time inside a line: the longest pause between its words in the middle 30–70 %, or None"""
    a, b = c["start"], c["end"]
    if b - a < 2.5: return None
    ws = [w for w in words if a - 0.2 <= w["start"] and w["end"] <= b + 0.2]
    gaps = [(n["start"] - p["end"], (p["end"] + n["start"]) / 2) for p, n in zip(ws, ws[1:])
            if a + 0.3 * (b - a) <= (p["end"] + n["start"]) / 2 <= a + 0.7 * (b - a)]
    return max(gaps)[1] if gaps else a + (b - a) / 2

class Scorer:
    """forced decoding: log-probability of a given text for an audio window, after a prompt"""
    def __init__(self, model, x, sr, lang, prompt):
        import whisper
        self.model, self.x, self.sr, self.whisper = model, x, sr, whisper
        self.tok = whisper.tokenizer.get_tokenizer(True, num_languages=model.num_languages, language=lang, task="transcribe")
        self.pre = ([self.tok.sot_prev] + self.tok.encode(" " + prompt) if prompt else []) + list(self.tok.sot_sequence_including_notimestamps)
        self.lang, self.prompt = lang, prompt
    def mel(self, a, b):
        seg = self.x[int(max(0, a - 0.2) * self.sr):int((b + 0.2) * self.sr)]
        return self.whisper.log_mel_spectrogram(self.whisper.pad_or_trim(seg), n_mels=self.model.dims.n_mels).to("cuda")
    def score(self, a, b, texts):
        import torch
        with torch.no_grad():
            feat = self.model.embed_audio(self.mel(a, b)[None])
            res = []
            for t in texts:
                seq = self.pre + self.tok.encode(t) + [self.tok.eot]
                lg = self.model.logits(torch.tensor([seq], device="cuda"), feat)[0].float().log_softmax(-1)
                res.append(sum(lg[i, seq[i + 1]].item() for i in range(len(self.pre) - 1, len(seq) - 1)))
        return res
    def decode(self, a, b, prompt):
        r = self.model.transcribe(self.x[int(max(0, a - 0.2) * self.sr):int((b + 0.2) * self.sr)], language=self.lang,
                                  initial_prompt=prompt, condition_on_previous_text=False, fp16=True, beam_size=5, best_of=5)
        return r["text"].strip()

def context(sents, i, before=2.5, after=1.5, longest=26.0):
    """-> (start, end, text before, text after): the line's window widened to the neighbours spoken within reach"""
    c = sents[i]; a, b = c["start"], c["end"]; pre = post = ""
    for k in range(i - 1, -1, -1):
        p = sents[k]
        if p["end"] < c["start"] - before or b - p["start"] > longest: break
        a = p["start"]; pre = p["text"] + pre
    for k in range(i + 1, len(sents)):
        n = sents[k]
        if n["start"] > c["end"] + after or n["end"] - a > longest: break
        b = n["end"]; post += n["text"]
    strip = lambda t: re.sub(r"[、。，．,.!?！？…\s]", "", t)
    return a, b, strip(pre), strip(post)

        missing = [k for nm, k in names.items() if re.search(r"\b%s\b" % nm, en) and k not in c["text"]]
        spans = [(k, sp) for k in missing for sp in name_spans(c["text"], k)]
        if missing: why.append("name " + "/".join(missing))
        core = cores[i]
        if numbers_en(core) - numbers_ja(c["text"]): why.append("number " + "/".join(str(v) for v in sorted(numbers_en(core) - numbers_ja(c["text"]))))
        if core.strip() and base[i] < XC_SIM: why.append("meaning %.2f" % base[i])
        if why: todo.append((i, why, missing, spans))
    model = load_model(model_name) if todo else None
    sc = Scorer(model, x, sr, lang, prompt) if model else None
    prog = Progress("cross-check line", len(todo)) if todo else None
    for n_, (i, why, missing, spans) in enumerate(todo):
        c = sents[i]; key = "v3|%.2f-%.2f|%s" % (c["start"], c["end"], c["text"])
        if key not in cache:
            cands = []
            for k, (sim, surf) in spans:
                cands.append(("name", c["text"].replace(surf, k, 1)))
            for p in ((prompt, None) if "\n" not in c["text"] else ()):
                t = sc.decode(c["start"], c["end"], p).rstrip(cfg["strip_end"])
                if t and t != c["text"] and difflib.SequenceMatcher(None, bare(t, cfg), bare(c["text"], cfg)).ratio() >= 0.4 \
                        and 0.6 <= len(bare(t, cfg)) / max(1, len(bare(c["text"], cfg))) <= 1.6:
                    cands.append(("decode", t))
            cut = halves(c, words) if "\n" not in c["text"] else None
            if cut is not None:
                # a line decoded whole can repeat its mishearing every time (過労死で); each half on its own window
                # hears it afresh (かろうじて), and the two halves joined are one more candidate
                t = (sc.decode(c["start"], cut, prompt) + sc.decode(cut, c["end"], prompt)).rstrip(cfg["strip_end"])
                if t and bare(t, cfg) != bare(c["text"], cfg) and difflib.SequenceMatcher(None, bare(t, cfg), bare(c["text"], cfg)).ratio() >= 0.4 \
                        and 0.6 <= len(bare(t, cfg)) / max(1, len(bare(c["text"], cfg))) <= 1.6:
                    cands.append(("halves", t))
            cands = list(dict.fromkeys(cands))
            # scored in context: a candidate decoded on this line's own window is Whisper's best for exactly that window,
            # so scoring it there favours it by construction (Terminator, 40:13: 知られては → 死なるとは, Δ +1.45);
            # embedded between its neighbours and scored over their audio, both texts compete on equal terms
            a_, b_, pre, post = context(sents, i)
            lps = sc.score(a_, b_, [pre + bare(c["text"], cfg) + post] + [pre + bare(t, cfg) + post for _, t in cands])
            cache[key] = dict(cur=lps[0], cands=[[kind, t, lp] for (kind, t), lp in zip(cands, lps[1:])])
        r = cache[key]; best = None
        for kind, t, lp in r["cands"]:
            gain = lp - r["cur"]
            if kind == "name":
                # the name must take the place of about as much text as it is: a span swallowing a word besides
                # (Terminator, 24:14: もう待った → マット, Δ +1.43) wins only because fewer tokens score better
                ok = gain >= XC_NAME_MARGIN and len(bare(t, cfg)) >= len(bare(c["text"], cfg)) - 1
            else:
                # only the original's own word for it counts: the name it says, or the number (see above) — and, as for a
                # name span, no text lost on the way: forced decoding scores every token, so fewer score better, and a
                # line that brings the name while dropping a word wins by the word (The Matrix, 1:39:37:
                # 頑張れ、モフィアス → モーフィアス, Δ +18.60)
                brings = [k for k in missing if k in t] or (numbers_en(cores[i]) & (numbers_ja(t) - numbers_ja(c["text"])))
                ok = gain >= XC_MARGIN and bool(brings) and len(bare(t, cfg)) >= len(bare(c["text"], cfg)) - 1
            if ok and (best is None or lp > best[2]): best = (kind, t, lp)
        if best:
            rep["fixes"].append((c["start"], c["end"], c["text"], best[1], best[0], best[2] - r["cur"], ", ".join(why), ens[i]))
            c["text"] = best[1]
        else:
            rep["suspects"].append((c["start"], c["end"], c["text"], ", ".join(why), ens[i],
                                    [(t, lp - r["cur"]) for _, t, lp in r["cands"]]))
        prog.step(n_ + 1, "%d corrected" % len(rep["fixes"]))
        if n_ % 20 == 0: json.dump(cache, open(cache_path, "w"), ensure_ascii=False)
    if prog: prog.done()
    json.dump(cache, open(cache_path, "w"), ensure_ascii=False)
    if model is not None: unload_model(model)
    del emb
    print("  cross-check: %d suspect lines, %d corrected by the audio, %d left for review"
          % (len(todo), len(rep["fixes"]), len(rep["suspects"])), flush=True)
    return rep

*** Verification against the original's subtitles
The VAD is not to be trusted with the dialogue of an action scene:
under gunfire, rotors and score it hears nothing, and without a
reference cue there Whisper is never asked (Mission: Impossible III,
2026-10-18: 319 of 365 English lines without a Japanese one had no VAD
speech under them; run on those cue windows alone, Whisper heard
nearly every one).  So the film's own subtitles in the original
language — the check track, by default the fullest text track in the
language of another audio stream — are the list of what must be
spoken: every speech cue of it that no kept Whisper segment covers by
=CHECK_MIN= is transcribed again on its own (padded, neighbours within
=CHECK_JOIN= in one window, unprompted, cached in
=segments_rescue.json=).  What Whisper hears there passes the usual
filters except the VAD's silence test — the VAD is what failed — and
only the words that no kept segment already holds are added.  After
the assembly the check is run once more against the written cues, and
whatever is still without a line goes into the review with its
original text — a dub sometimes leaves a line out, but that is for a
human to see, not to be lost silently.

CHECK_MIN, CHECK_JOIN = 0.25, 1.0   # a check cue counts as voiced when lines cover this share of it; missing cues this close share one window
CHECK_WARN = 0.03                   # more than this share of check cues still without a line is reported loudly

def unvoiced(check, spans):
    """check cues that the spans cover by less than CHECK_MIN"""
    spans = sorted(spans); starts = [a for a, _ in spans]
    out = []
    for s, e, t in check:
        near = spans[bisect.bisect_left(starts, s - MAX_REGION - 2):bisect.bisect_right(starts, e)]
        if sum(max(0.0, min(e, b) - max(s, a)) for a, b in near) < CHECK_MIN*max(e - s, 0.01): out.append((s, e, t))
    return out

def rescue(x, sr, segs, check, lang, model_name, cfg, cache):
    """-> segments plus what Whisper hears in the check cues left without one, the rescues [(start, end, check text, new text)], the dropped"""
    miss = unvoiced(check, [(g["start"], g["end"]) for g in segs])
    if not miss: return segs, [], []
    total = len(x)/sr; wins = []
    for s, e, t in miss:
        if wins and s - wins[-1][1] <= CHECK_JOIN and e - wins[-1][0] <= MAX_REGION - PAD_BEFORE - PAD_AFTER:
            wins[-1][1] = e; wins[-1][2].append(t)
        else: wins.append([s, e, [t]])
    regions = [[max(0.0, s - PAD_BEFORE), min(total, e + PAD_AFTER)] for s, e, _ in wins]
    print("  check: %d of %d check cues without a line, %d windows transcribed on their own" % (len(miss), len(check), len(wins)), flush=True)
    new = transcribe(x, sr, regions, lang, model_name, None, cache, label="rescue window")
    spans = [(g["start"], g["end"]) for g in segs]; added = []; rescues = []; dropped = []
    for k, (s, e, texts) in enumerate(wins):
        got = []
        for h in (h for h in new if h["region"] == k):
            why = bad_reason(h, cfg, None)
            if why: dropped.append((h, "rescue: " + why)); continue
            words = [w for w in h["words"] if w["w"].strip() and not any(a <= (w["start"] + w["end"])/2 <= b for a, b in spans)]
            if not words or not re.search(r"\w", "".join(w["w"] for w in words)): continue
            g = dict(h, words=words, start=words[0]["start"], end=words[-1]["end"], text="".join(w["w"] for w in words), region=-1, rescued=True)
            got.append(g); spans.append((g["start"], g["end"]))
        added += got
        rescues.append((s, e, " / ".join(t.replace("\n", " ") for t in texts), "".join(g["text"].strip() for g in got)))
    out = sorted(segs + added, key=lambda g: g["start"])
    return out, rescues, dropped

def uncovered(cues, vad):
    cov = np.zeros(int(max([c[1] for c in cues] + [1])*10)+20)
    for c in cues: cov[int(c[0]*10):int(c[1]*10)+1] = 1
    return [(s, e) for s, e in vad if e-s >= 0.8 and int(e*10) < len(cov) and cov[int(s*10):int(e*10)].mean() < 0.3]

def worth_a_listen(x):
    """a suspect missing a name or number, or with a candidate the audio nearly took; the rest is mostly paraphrase"""
    return "name" in x[3] or "number" in x[3] or any(g >= -3 for _, g in x[5])

**** The corrections review as subtitles
The same cross-check, filtered to what a person should hear, as an SRT
beside the input: =INPUT-basename.possible-LANG-CORRECTIONS-review.srt=.
A player that picks up subtitles by the film's name offers it as one
more track, so the review is done by watching — each line at its own
moment, with the audio under it, instead of seeking timestamps from a
text file.  It is written beside the input on every run with the
cross-check, and stays there when =-M= moves the SRT and the text
review into the cache: it is a file for a person, the cache is not.

Two kinds of cue, nothing else: the lines the audio corrected (=[直]=,
the new text and what it replaced, since an automatic correction is
the thing most worth doubting) and the suspects worth a listen (=[?]=,
the line, the best candidate the audio did not take, and why it is
suspect).  The lines merely far in meaning — mostly the dub
paraphrasing — are left to the text review.  Each cue ends with the
original's line, cut to a readable length.

def write_review_srt(path, xc, near):
    def en(t): t = re.sub(r"\s+", " ", t).strip(); return "EN: " + (t if len(t) <= 110 else t[:107] + "...")
    cues = []
    for s, e, old, new, kind, gain, why, eng in xc["fixes"]:
        cues.append((s, e, "[直] %s\n← %s  (%s, Δ %+.1f)\n%s" % (new.replace("\n", " / "), old.replace("\n", " / "), why, gain, en(eng))))
    for s, e, t, why, eng, cands in xc["suspects"]:
        if not near((s, e, t, why, eng, cands)): continue
        best = max(cands, key=lambda c: c[1]) if cands else None
        cues.append((s, e, "[?] %s  (%s)\n%s%s" % (t.replace("\n", " / "), why, "候補: %s  (Δ %+.1f)\n" % (best[0], best[1]) if best else "", en(eng))))
    cues.sort()
    for k in range(len(cues) - 1):                     # a player shows overlapping cues stacked; keep them in turn
        if cues[k][1] > cues[k + 1][0]: cues[k] = (cues[k][0], max(cues[k][0] + 0.3, cues[k + 1][0]), cues[k][2])
    write_srt(path, cues)
    return len(cues)

def ffprobe_streams(path):

    out = subprocess.run(["ffprobe", "-v", "error", "-show_entries",

def scan_subtitle_cues(path):
    import subprocess
    out = subprocess.run(["ffprobe", "-v", "error", "-select_streams", "s", "-show_entries",
                          "packet=stream_index,pts_time,duration_time,size", "-of", "csv=p=0", path],
                         capture_output=True, text=True).stdout
    pk = {}
    for line in out.splitlines():
        f = line.split(",")
        if len(f) < 4 or f[1] in ("N/A", ""): continue
        pk.setdefault(int(f[0]), []).append((float(f[1]), None if f[2] in ("N/A", "") else float(f[2]), int(f[3])))
    cues = {}
    for idx, lst in pk.items():
        lst.sort(); c = []
        for i, (t, d, size) in enumerate(lst):
            if size <= 200 and d is None: continue                       # PGS clear set
            nxt = lst[i+1][0] if i+1 < len(lst) else t + 3.0
            end = t + d if d else nxt
            if end > nxt: end = nxt
            if end - t >= 0.2: c.append((t, end))
        cues[idx] = c
    return cues

def text_subtitle_cues(path, subs, work):

**** timeline — is the reference SRT on the audio's timeline?
Cross-correlates a 10 Hz "some cue is on" signal with the VAD's "someone
speaks" signal over ±180 s.  A peak near 0 means the same timeline;
otherwise the offset is what =-s= needs.
#+begin_src python
def probe_timeline(cues, vad, total):
    """-> offset in seconds, or None without a reference"""
    if not cues: print("timeline: no reference SRT given (-r)"); return None
    n = int(total*10)+1; a = np.zeros(n); b = np.zeros(n)
    for s, e, _ in cues: a[int(s*10):int(e*10)+1] = 1
    for s, e in vad: b[int(s*10):int(e*10)+1] = 1
    a -= a.mean(); b -= b.mean()
    from scipy.signal import fftconvolve
    corr = fftconvolve(b, a[::-1], mode="full")            # index n-1 <-> lag 0
    lags = np.arange(-n+1, n)/10.0; sel = np.abs(lags) <= 180
    c = corr[sel]; l = lags[sel]; i = int(np.argmax(c)); peak = c[i]/(np.sqrt((a**2).sum()*(b**2).sum())+1e-9)
    print("timeline: best offset (audio speech minus reference cues) = %+.1f s, correlation %.2f" % (l[i], peak))
    print("          first cue %s, first VAD speech %s" % (hms(cues[0][0]), hms(vad[0][0]) if vad else "-"))
    if abs(l[i]) >= 0.3: print("          -> the reference is on another timeline: use  -s %+.1f" % l[i])
    else: print("          -> same timeline, no -s needed")
    return float(l[i])

def parse_names(spec):
    """'正=誤1|誤2,名前' -> {canonical: [variants]}"""

*** Decode the audio
Any container or codec =ffmpeg= reads; =-a N= selects the audio track
of a video file.  16 kHz mono is what Whisper and the VAD want.

The WAV must start at the container's time 0, not at the stream's
first packet: a track added later with =mkvmerge --sync= (a dub taken
from another disc) starts seconds in, and a plain decode drops that
lead-in — the whole transcription then runs early by exactly that much
(Terminator, 2026-10-17: 8.15 s).  =-copyts= keeps the packets'
absolute times — the ones the cue scan of the subtitle tracks reads —
and =aresample=async=1:first_pts=0= fills everything before the first
packet with silence; without =-copyts= ffmpeg would count from the
container's start, which in a stream-only =.mka= is the late track's
own first packet.  A stamp records how and from which stream the
WAV was made; when it differs, or the input is newer, the WAV is
decoded again, and when the audio then differs every cache derived
from it is dropped, since all of them hold its times.  (=-M= rewrites
the input, which makes it newer: the audio comes out the same and the
transcription is kept.)  The
padding is checked, not trusted: the WAV must reach the end of the
stream's last packet within 1 s, else the run stops.

Only the dub's centre channel is decoded when the stream has one
(=-c auto=, the default): a film mix puts the dialogue there and the
effects and score around it, and a downmix of all channels buries the
voices exactly where the action is loudest — the VAD then hears no
speech and Whisper, given the window anyway, writes its stock outro
phrases (Mission: Impossible III, 13:50–25:30: 49 of 117 English lines
heard by the VAD in the downmix, 67 in the centre channel; Whisper's
no-speech probability on them fell from ≈ 0.6 to ≈ 0.35).  A centre
channel that turns out silent (peak below -50 dB) falls back to the
downmix; =-c mix= forces the downmix, =-c center= insists on the centre.
#+begin_src bash
WAV="$WORK/audio16k.wav"; STAMP="$WORK/audio16k.stamp"

then
    # an untagged stream (a dub muxed in from another source: Terminator 2's 6-channel AC-3 reads "unknown");
    # the decoder's order for 3, 6 and 8 channels puts the centre third
    NCH="$(ffprobe -v error -select_streams a:$ASTREAM -show_entries stream=channels -of csv=p=0 "$INPUT" | head -1)"
    case "$NCH" in
        3 ) LAYOUT="3.0" ;;
        6 ) LAYOUT="5.1(side)" ;;
        8 ) LAYOUT="7.1" ;;
    esac
    case "$LAYOUT" in 3.0|"5.1(side)"|7.1 ) FCSEL="c2" ;; esac
fi

then
    CH="center"
elif [ "$CHANNEL" = "center" ]
then
    echo "Error: -c center, but audio stream $ASTREAM (layout '${LAYOUT:-unknown}') has no centre channel"; exit 1
fi

if [ ! -f "$WAV" ] || [ "$INPUT" -nt "$WAV" ] || [ "$(cat "$STAMP" 2>/dev/null)" != "$STAMPV" ]
then
    echo "-------------------------"
    echo "Decoding audio stream $ASTREAM of $INPUT to 16 kHz mono ($([ "$CH" = center ] && echo "its centre channel, the dialogue" || echo "a downmix of all channels")) ..."
    echo "-------------------------"
    NEW="$WORK/audio16k.new.wav"
    PAN=""; [ "$CH" = "center" ] && PAN=",pan=mono|c0=$FCSEL"

       awk -v p="$(ffmpeg -nostdin -hide_banner -i "$NEW" -af volumedetect -f null - 2>&1 | sed -n 's/.*max_volume: \(-*[0-9.]*\) dB.*/\1/p')" 'BEGIN { exit !(p == "" || p < -50) }'
    then
        echo "  the centre channel is silent: decoding the downmix instead"

    fi
    TEND=$(ffprobe -v error -select_streams a:$ASTREAM -show_entries packet=pts_time,duration_time -of csv=p=0 "$INPUT" \
               | awk -F, '$1 != "N/A" { e = $1 + ($2 == "N/A" ? 0 : $2); if (e > m) m = e } END { print m }')
    TWAV=$(ffprobe -v error -show_entries format=duration -of csv=p=0 "$NEW")

    then

        echo "       its lead-in was lost and every line would run early."

    fi
    echo "$STAMPV" > "$STAMP"
fi
